# Code of Conduct

> English. Tiếng Việt: [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.vi.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.vi.md)

gpupool adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its Code of Conduct. That text is the governing document and applies in full; this page is a short summary in our
own words and does not replace it.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
- Assume good faith, disagree about ideas and code rather than about people, and accept feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, and learn from them.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful to the project and its community.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments, including about a person's identity or background.
- Sexualized language or attention, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the
project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer, Long Duong
([@longduongbao29](https://github.com/longduongbao29)):

- use GitHub's private "Report a vulnerability" form on the
  [Security tab](https://github.com/longduongbao29/gpupool/security/advisories/new) and start the
  description with "Code of Conduct", or
- contact the maintainer through the contact options on the GitHub profile.

Reports are handled privately and in good faith, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential as far as
possible. Maintainers may respond with a warning, temporary or permanent removal of contributions, comments or
access, or a ban from the project spaces, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
